Privacy & support

ArsCorp · Web version · Updated October 17, 2026

This page describes the published browser version of Cap Stack Sort.

What the game saves

Your best score, last completed game, tutorial choice and sound/animation settings are stored in this browser’s local storage. Creator Studio also stores scenes locally when you choose Save. They are not sent to an ArsCorp account or game server.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

The current game has no account registration, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or in-game payments. It does not ask for your location, microphone, camera, contacts or personal profile.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Scenes and sharing

Downloading a scene creates a JSON file on your device. A copied scene link includes its name, board, Next stacks and playback choices in the URL fragment. Anyone you give that link to can read and replay the scene. Saving a scene in the browser keeps it local.

Creator video export

When you choose Record & download, the browser records the game artwork, live cap positions, score animation and synthesized game sounds locally. This tool does not access your microphone, camera or other tabs. The downloaded MP4 or WebM stays on your device until you choose to share or delete it.

Keeping and clearing data

Browser data stays until you clear this website’s storage or your browser removes it. You can remove it through your browser’s site-data controls. This also removes your saved game and best score. A downloaded JSON file stays until you delete it yourself.

Support

You can contact the project maintainer through the Cap Stack Sort support tracker. Posting an issue is public, so avoid including private information. Browsing those external sites is covered by their own privacy policies.

A future store release with advertising, payments, analytics or online accounts will need updated disclosures covering those features.
