Privacy & support

ArsCorp · Mobile edition · Updated October 17, 2026

This page describes the bundled Android/iOS edition of Cap Stack Sort by ArsCorp.

What the game saves

Your best score, last completed game, tutorial choice and sound/animation settings are stored in the application’s local WebView storage. They are not sent to an ArsCorp account or game server.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

This edition bundles the game locally and has no account registration, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or in-game payments. The Capacitor App plugin handles background/foreground and Android Back events. Game progress stays on this device and is not synchronized between devices. It does not ask for your location, microphone, camera, contacts or personal profile.

Keeping and clearing data

App data stays until you clear the application data or uninstall the app. You can remove it through Android’s app storage settings or by deleting the iOS app. This also removes your saved game and best score. Creator Studio is not included in this edition.

Support

You can contact the project maintainer through the Cap Stack Sort support tracker. Posting an issue is public, so avoid including private information. Browsing those external sites is covered by their own privacy policies.

Policy scope: edition 2.2.1. Data practices must be reviewed before introducing any new SDK, payments or accounts.
