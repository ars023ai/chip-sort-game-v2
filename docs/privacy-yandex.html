Privacy & support

ArsCorp · Yandex Games edition · Updated October 17, 2026

This page describes the Yandex Games edition of Cap Stack Sort by ArsCorp.

What the game saves

Your best score, last completed game, tutorial choice and sound/animation settings are stored in this browser’s local storage. They are not sent to an ArsCorp account or game server.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

This edition uses the Yandex Games SDK for platform loading and gameplay events and full-screen advertising between completed rounds. ArsCorp does not run its own account system, analytics server or in-game payments. Yandex and its advertising partners may process device, browser, advertising identifiers and interaction information under their own policies. Consult Yandex Privacy Policy and the privacy controls offered by the platform. Game progress remains in local storage and is not synchronized between devices. It does not ask for your location, microphone, camera, contacts or personal profile.

Keeping and clearing data

Browser data stays until you clear this website’s storage or your browser removes it. You can remove it through your browser’s site-data controls. This also removes your saved game and best score. A downloaded JSON file stays until you delete it yourself.

Support

You can contact the project maintainer through the Cap Stack Sort support tracker. Posting an issue is public, so avoid including private information. Browsing those external sites is covered by their own privacy policies.

Policy scope: edition 2.2.0. Data practices must be reviewed before introducing any new SDK, payments or accounts.
